HALO NATIVE PARTICLE EDITOR BATCH

Open these PCFs from addons/VJ-Halo/particles in a fresh GMod -tools session.
Supported particle compositions; explicit native-only exclusions are listed in SKIPPED.txt.
New effects and pivot corrections need whole-effect comparison against their named Halo source.
37 roster effects retain their accepted definitions and dependency bytes.
Smoke/blood uses the approved native ambient/emission lighting approximation.
54 native effect bindings installed for gameplay testing; visual verification pending. No CER packs were retired; uncovered routes retain their existing effects.
CP0 is the effect origin; +X is forward. Flight/bolt/tracer effects need a moving CP0.
Owner-bound emitters must be stopped by their owner; particles then finish their own lifetimes.
Nonzero source sockets and scene lights/decals are listed in inventory.json for integration.
